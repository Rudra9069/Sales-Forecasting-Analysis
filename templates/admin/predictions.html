How It Works

Understanding the forecasting approach

1

Aggregation — Historical transaction data is aggregated to calculate total revenue per month.

2

Model Choice — A Linear Regression model analyzes the month-over-month trend across the dataset.

3

Projection — The requested future month is projected using the calculated historical trend slope.

4

Limitation — Currently, only 4 months of data exist, making the forecast highly sensitive to recent anomalies.
