Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Forecastify platform features.

How does Forecastify calculate sales predictions?
Forecastify analyzes historical order frequency, category purchasing trends, and daily transaction volume to generate short-term and long-term revenue forecasts.
Is data synchronized in real-time?
Yes! Every order, user registration, and stock adjustment immediately updates the database and live admin metrics automatically.
Can I export reports for external accounting?
Absolutely. The admin dashboard features a single-click "Export Master Report (.CSV)" button that generates structured financial reports containing orders, product metrics, and customer details.
